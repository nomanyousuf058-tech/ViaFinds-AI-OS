# 22 PAGE ROUTE REGISTRY

Verified from `app/` directory structure.

## Public Frontend Routes
- **`/` (`app/page.tsx`)**: Homepage.
- **`/[...slug]/`**: Dynamic route for articles and pages.
- **`/articles/`**: Article index.
- **`/reviews/`**: Product review index.
- **`/category/`**: Category taxonomy view.
- **`/brands/`**: Brand/Merchant taxonomy view.
- **`/search/`**: Search results page.

## Static/Legal Pages
- **`/about/`**: About ViaFinds.
- **`/contact/`**: Contact form.
- **`/privacy-policy/`**: Privacy policy.
- **`/terms-of-service/`**: TOS.
- **`/cookie-policy/`**: GDPR/CCPA cookie information.
- **`/affiliate-disclosure/`**: FTC affiliate disclosure.

## Feeds & Technical
- **`/rss.xml`**: RSS Feed.
- **`robots.ts` & `sitemap.ts`**: SEO bot directives.

## Admin Portal
- **`/login/`**: Admin authentication.
- **`/dashboard/`**: Overview statistics.
- **`/dashboard/articles/`**: Article management and review.
- **`/dashboard/automation/`**: Triggering new AI pipelines.
- **`/dashboard/jobs/`**: Queue and error monitoring.
- **`/dashboard/optimization/`**: SEO/EEAT recommendations.
- **`/dashboard/services/`**: Integration management.
