# 34 AI BRAIN HYBRID BUSINESS MODEL

## Purpose
Managing the conflict and synergy between Owned Products and Affiliate Products.

## The Conflict
If ViaFinds sells its own "Productivity Course" ($50 profit), but also promotes an affiliate "Productivity Masterclass" ($100 commission), which does the Brain prioritize?

## The Hybrid Strategy Engine
The Brain must objectively evaluate:
1. **Financial**: Expected Value = (Commission/Profit) * Conversion Rate.
2. **Brand Trust**: If ViaFinds only pushes its own products, it loses the "unbiased editorial" authority that drives SEO traffic.
3. **Intent Matching**: 
   - User searches "ViaFinds Productivity Course Review" $\rightarrow$ Show Owned Product.
   - User searches "Best Productivity Courses 2026" $\rightarrow$ Show Affiliate Competitors, and place the Owned Product as "Our Top Pick" or "Best Value".

## Evidence-Based Recommendations
The Brain is forbidden from hardcoding a preference for Owned Products. It must run an A/B test (Experiment Engine) to prove that the Owned Product yields a higher EPC before executing a site-wide CTA swap.
