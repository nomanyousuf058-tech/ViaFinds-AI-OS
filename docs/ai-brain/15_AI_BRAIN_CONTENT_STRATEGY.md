# 15 AI BRAIN CONTENT STRATEGY

## Purpose
The Brain must dynamically choose the correct article format based on intent, competition, and business strategy. It must NOT default to treating every product as a generic "Review".

## Format Types
The Brain can select from (but is not limited to):
- **Review**: Deep dive into a single product.
- **Comparison**: "Product X vs Product Y".
- **Buying Guide**: "Top 10 Tools for Z".
- **How-To / Tutorial**: "How to solve problem A using Product B".
- **Problem/Solution**: Focuses heavily on the pain point before introducing the product.
- **Case Study**: Simulated or researched real-world application.
- **Listicle**: Fast-paced, high-volume search intent.

## Selection Matrix
Selection is driven by data:
- **Search Intent**: If the user searches "best sleep trackers", the format MUST be a Buying Guide or Listicle. If they search "Oura ring accuracy", the format MUST be an Educational or Review piece.
- **Funnel Position**: Top-of-funnel (Educational), Middle-of-funnel (Comparison), Bottom-of-funnel (Review/Discount).
- **Competition**: If SERP is dominated by heavy 5000-word guides, the Brain might choose a contrarian "Short-form Feature" to stand out in Answer Engines (AEO).
