# 32 AI BRAIN OWNED PRODUCT SUPPORT

## Purpose
Preparing the Brain for the evolution from Affiliate-only to an Owned-Product business model (100% margin).

## Database Requirements
The existing `products` table needs expansion (or a new table `owned_products` must be created) to support:
- `product_type`: 'owned'
- `delivery_mechanism`: 'digital_download', 'course_access'
- `checkout_url`: Link to Stripe or internal checkout.

## Brain Workflows for Owned Products

### 1. The Identification Workflow
The Brain analyzes high-traffic, high-intent articles that are currently monetizing poorly via affiliates. It generates an **Implementation Request** suggesting the Admin create a specific owned product to fill the gap.

### 2. The Promotion Workflow
When an Owned Product is added to the database:
- Brain reads the product features and target audience.
- Brain scans existing articles for semantic matches.
- Brain queues jobs to dynamically inject CTAs for the Owned Product into legacy affiliate articles.

### 3. The Content Flywheel
The Brain designs a new topical cluster specifically engineered to drive traffic to the Owned Product, utilizing Educational and How-To formats rather than standard Reviews.
